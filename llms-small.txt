# Aleph Printing & Graphics
Aleph is a Lebanese printing house founded 1972 in Beirut. Specializes in luxury packaging, labels, pharmaceutical/food/cosmetic printing, security printing, book publishing, and commercial print. Processes 150 tons/month.
