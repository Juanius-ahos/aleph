About
Aleph

Founded in 1972, Aleph is one of the first printing houses established in Lebanon. The company was originally specialized in color separation and reproduction.

Following years of expertise, the company grew to a high level in labeling, publishing and commercial high-class printing. With the possibility to handle 150 tons per month Aleph answers to the demands of many different customers across Lebanon as well as abroad.

FAQ

Common Questions

Where is Aleph Printing located?

+

Street 41, Mekales, Beirut, Lebanon. Find us on Google maps: https://maps.app.goo.gl/MH2h2HQ5z6BZmf7f7

What year was Aleph founded?

+

1972 — making Aleph one of the very first printing houses in Lebanon, originally specialising in colour separation and reproduction.

What printing services does Aleph offer?

+

Full commercial printing: luxury rigid hard boxes, soft boxes, special packages, labels (food, beverage, pharma), brochures, leaflets, security envelopes, business cards, bags, binding, security printing, and in-house design.

How large is Aleph's printing capacity?

+

Aleph processes approximately 150 tons of paper per month — one of the largest commercial printing operations in Lebanon. With 37 employees Aleph is considered a medium size company.

Does Aleph do pharmaceutical printing?

+

Yes. Aleph produces pharmaceutical labels, package inserts, multi-language folded leaflets, and serialised packaging for pharmaceutical companies and distributors across Lebanon and the GCC.

Can Aleph print food packaging and food labels?

+

Yes. We produce food-safe labels on Water resistant paper and other substrates, folded cartons, and branded bags for food and beverage brands, meeting Lebanese and GCC food labelling regulations including bilingual Arabic/English requirements.

Does Aleph produce luxury packaging and rigid boxes?

+

Yes. Aleph produces luxury rigid hard boxes and soft boxes with premium finishes including hot-foil stamping, embossing, soft-touch lamination, and spot UV — used by cosmetic, jewellery, confectionery, and hospitality brands.

Does Aleph do security printing?

+

Yes. Aleph produces security envelopes, serialised controlled documents, anti-counterfeit labels, and high-security institutional packaging — with restricted access production and full chain-of-custody procedures.

Can I order custom packaging for my brand?

+

Yes. Contact us with your specifications — format, quantity, materials, and deadline — and our team will provide a custom quote within one business day.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for printing?

+

MOQs vary by product: labels typically from 1,000 units, carton boxes from 500 units, rigid boxes from 200–500 units depending on complexity. Contact us to discuss your specific requirement.

How long does printing take at Aleph?

+

Standard turnaround is 7–15 working days from artwork approval, depending on the product and quantity. Rush timelines can be discussed. Contact us for a project-specific schedule.

Does Aleph offer graphic design services?

+

Yes. We have an in-house graphic design team that can develop packaging artwork, brand identities, and print-ready files, as a standalone service or as part of a full print production brief.

Can Aleph print books and publications in Arabic?

+

Yes. Aleph is one of Lebanon's original Arabic print specialists and produces bilingual and Arabic-language books, catalogues, and publications including our own Edition Aleph contemporary Arabic publication series.

Does Aleph export its products outside Lebanon?

+

Yes. Aleph serves clients across Lebanon and exports to the GCC and broader MENA region, including Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, and Qatar.

Can I get a sample before committing to a full print run?

+

Yes, press proofs and physical samples are standard parts of our production process. Contact us to discuss sampling options for your project.

What types of paper and substrates does Aleph print on?

+

We print on coated and uncoated papers, metallized stocks, security substrates, carton board, and specialty papers. We offer Cotton paper from Fabriano, Conqueror and Fedrigoni. Our team will recommend the right substrate for your product type and end use.

Does Aleph produce cosmetic packaging?

+

Yes. We produce cosmetic labels, rigid boxes, folded cartons, and special packages for beauty and skincare brands, with premium finishes including soft-touch lamination, foil, embossing, and spot UV.

How do I get a quote from Aleph?

+

Email alephprint@aleph.com.lb, call +961 1 685 354/5, WhatsApp +961 70 425 152, or use the contact form on this page. Include your product type, quantity, format, and any artwork if available.
