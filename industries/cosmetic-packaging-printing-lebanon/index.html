Overview

In the beauty industry, packaging is product. Consumers judge quality, prestige, and authenticity by the tactile and visual experience before they even open the box. Aleph produces cosmetic packaging for local Lebanese brands and international labels distributed across the region — from minimalist skincare cartons to elaborate gift-set boxes with custom inserts. Our finishing capabilities (spot UV, soft-touch lamination, foil stamping, embossing) deliver the premium feel that beauty consumers expect.

Industry Challenges

The Printing Challenges You Face

Premium Tactile Experience

Cosmetic consumers equate packaging feel with product quality. Matt lamination, soft-touch coatings, and embossing must be applied consistently across every unit in the run — with zero tolerance for delamination or impression variation.

Complex Structures

Beauty packaging often involves multi-component boxes with internal trays, magnetic closures, ribbon pulls, and custom foam inserts — requiring die-cutting precision and structural engineering before a single sheet is printed.

Brand Colour Accuracy

A brand's signature colour is its most recognisable asset. Offset printing for cosmetics must match Pantone references exactly, batch after batch, across packaging types ranging from outer boxes to inner labels.

Aleph Solutions

How Aleph Solves Them

Rigid & Folding Carton Boxes

We produce rigid hard boxes and folded carton boxes for cosmetics, with custom sizes, internal structures, and premium surface finishes including spot UV, gloss, matt, and soft-touch lamination.

Premium Label Production

High-resolution offset labels on metallised, transparent, and textured stocks — with foil hot-stamping, embossing, and sequential numbering for limited-edition cosmetic collections.

Custom Die-Cut & Specialty Shapes

We engineer and produce bespoke die-cut packaging shapes including star boxes, hexagonal tubes, and asymmetric gift packaging — bringing cosmetic brand concepts to life in production volumes.

FAQ

Common Questions

Does Aleph offer soft-touch lamination for cosmetic packaging?

+

Yes. We apply soft-touch (velvet) lamination on offset-printed cartons and rigid boxes, delivering the premium tactile feel expected in luxury and mid-tier cosmetic packaging.

Can you produce limited-edition numbered cosmetic packaging?

+

Yes — we offer sequential numbering and variable data printing, suitable for limited-edition beauty collections, gift sets, and serialised luxury cosmetic products.

What is the minimum order for cosmetic rigid boxes?

+

Rigid hard boxes typically start from 500 units. Folded cartons can be ordered in smaller quantities. Contact us to discuss your collection size and timeline.

Can Aleph match our brand's Pantone colour exactly?

+

Pantone spot colour matching is standard in our offset production. We will provide press proofs for approval before committing to a full run.

Do you handle the full cosmetic packaging process including design?

+

Yes — we offer in-house graphic design services and can produce packaging from concept to finished product, including structural engineering for custom box shapes.
