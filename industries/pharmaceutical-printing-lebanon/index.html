Overview

Lebanon's pharmaceutical industry demands the highest standards in print accuracy, regulatory compliance, and traceability. From multi-language package inserts to serialised labels for export shipments, Aleph has been supplying pharmaceutical companies and distributors across Lebanon and the GCC since 1972. Our offset presses deliver the colour accuracy, micro-text clarity, and substrate flexibility that pharma printing requires — on time, every batch.

Industry Challenges

The Printing Challenges You Face

Regulatory Compliance

Pharmaceutical packaging must comply with Lebanon's Ministry of Public Health regulations, EU directives, and GCC import standards — requiring precise text, barcode placement, and multilingual content on the same label or leaflet.

Small-Batch Flexibility

Pharma runs vary enormously — from 500-unit clinical trial labels to million-unit commercial production. Print suppliers must handle both without compromising quality or turnaround speed.

Substrate & Ink Safety

Pharmaceutical labels must be printed with inks that resist solvents, moisture, and cold-chain temperatures, on substrates that adhere reliably to glass, plastic, and foil packaging.

Aleph Solutions

How Aleph Solves Them

Multi-Language Label Production

We produce labels in Arabic, English, French, and custom combinations on a single offset run, with exact colour matching across all language variants. Variable barcode and serial number printing available.

Folded Package Inserts & Leaflets

High-volume folded leaflets printed on thin, pharmaceutical-grade paper with hairline text clarity. We handle complex fold sequences required by blister packs, vials, and ampoule packaging.

Tamper-Evident & Security Features

Aleph produces packaging with void labels, sequential serialisation, security holograms, and controlled-document printing — reducing counterfeiting risk for branded pharmaceutical products.

FAQ

Common Questions

Does Aleph produce pharmaceutical labels compliant with Lebanese regulations?

+

Yes. We work with pharmaceutical companies and distributors to produce labels that meet the Ministry of Public Health's specifications — including mandatory Arabic text, batch numbers, expiry dates, and barcode placements.

Can you print multi-language pharmaceutical leaflets in one run?

+

Absolutely. We produce folded package inserts with multiple languages (Arabic, English, French) in a single offset run, ensuring consistent ink coverage and legible micro-text on pharmaceutical-grade paper stocks.

What is the minimum order quantity for pharmaceutical labels?

+

We can accommodate runs from 500 units for clinical trial or pilot phases up to multi-million-unit commercial production. Contact us to discuss your batch size and turnaround requirements.

Do you offer serialised or variable-data printing for pharma?

+

Yes — we offer sequential numbering, QR codes, and variable barcode printing suitable for track-and-trace requirements in both the Lebanese and GCC markets.

What substrates are suitable for pharmaceutical labels?

+

We print on paper, BOPP, PET, and metallic substrates using solvent-resistant inks, suitable for glass, PVC, and aluminium packaging surfaces typical in pharma.
