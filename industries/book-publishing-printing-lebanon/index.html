Overview

Lebanon has a rich publishing tradition — from Arabic literary works and bilingual academic texts to high-end art books and commercial catalogues distributed across the Arab world. Aleph has served publishers, agencies, institutions, and independent authors since 1972, combining industrial offset capacity with the craftsmanship that books require. Our Edition Aleph imprint demonstrates what our presses can produce at their finest — and we bring the same standards to every publishing brief we receive.

Industry Challenges

The Printing Challenges You Face

Colour Fidelity Across Pages

Art books, photography catalogues, and illustrated publications require consistent colour reproduction across hundreds of pages — any drift between signatures is immediately visible and commercially damaging.

Binding Integrity

A book that falls apart is a failed product. Saddle stitch, perfect binding, and hardcover case binding each require different technical expertise — and the wrong choice of adhesive or thread for a given paper stock causes failures in the field.

Arabic Typography & Bidirectionality

Publishing in Arabic requires a print supplier who understands right-to-left layout, Arabic font rendering, and the unique spacing and kerning requirements that affect readability — especially in academic and children's publishing.

Aleph Solutions

How Aleph Solves Them

High-Fidelity Offset Printing

Our offset presses deliver accurate CMYK reproduction on coated and uncoated stocks, with consistent ink density across the full run — essential for photography books, art catalogues, and colour-critical publications.

Full Binding Range

We offer saddle stitch (up to 64 pages), perfect binding (square spine paperbacks), spiral/wire-o binding, and hardcover case binding with cloth, paper, or leatherette covers — all produced in-house in Beirut.

Arabic & Bilingual Publishing

Aleph is one of Lebanon's original Arabic print specialists. We handle right-to-left composition, Arabic numeral pagination, and mixed Arabic/Latin layouts — including the Edition Aleph series of contemporary Arabic publications.

FAQ

Common Questions

What binding options does Aleph offer for book printing?

+

We offer saddle stitch (stapled), perfect binding (square spine), wire-o/spiral, and hardcover case binding. The right choice depends on your page count, paper weight, and intended use.

Can Aleph print bilingual Arabic/English books?

+

Yes — we have extensive experience with bilingual and multilingual publishing, including right-to-left Arabic text, mixed layouts, and Arabic typography. Edition Aleph, our own publication series, is printed in-house.

What is the minimum print run for books?

+

Offset printing is economical from around 500 copies upward. For shorter runs, we can discuss alternatives. Contact us with your page count, format, and desired quantity for a quote.

Can you produce art books with accurate colour reproduction?

+

Yes. Our offset presses are calibrated for high-fidelity CMYK reproduction on coated art papers, delivering the colour accuracy required for photography books, exhibition catalogues, and fine art publications.

Do you handle the complete book production process?

+

We handle printing and binding. We work alongside your designer or can involve our in-house design team for cover and layout work. Final pre-press and proofing are standard parts of our production process.
