Overview

Luxury packaging communicates value before a word is read or a product is touched. For high-end retail, hospitality, jewellery, spirits, and premium gift brands operating in Lebanon and across the GCC, Aleph produces packaging that commands attention and conveys exclusivity. Our expertise in premium finishes — gold and silver hot-foil stamping, blind embossing, spot UV lacquer, soft-touch lamination — combined with the structural precision of rigid box construction, delivers packaging that becomes part of the product experience itself.

Industry Challenges

The Printing Challenges You Face

Consistent Premium Quality

Luxury brands cannot tolerate variation. Every box in a run of 10,000 must be identical — same emboss depth, same foil adhesion, same colour — because a single sub-standard unit reaching a consumer damages brand equity.

Custom Structure & Engineering

Luxury packaging is rarely standard. Brands need custom dimensions, magnetic closures, ribbon lifts, internal trays, and multi-layer structures that require engineering expertise before production begins.

Material Selection

The weight, texture, and finish of every substrate — board, paper, fabric lining, foam insert — contributes to the premium experience. Sourcing the right combination of materials for each brief is a specialist skill.

Aleph Solutions

How Aleph Solves Them

Rigid Hard Box Production

We manufacture rigid clamshell, lid-and-base, and slipcase boxes in custom sizes with premium surface finishes. Our rigid boxes are used by Lebanese jewellers, confectionery brands, spirits distributors, and hospitality groups.

Premium Surface Finishing

Our finishing capabilities include gold/silver/coloured hot-foil stamping, blind and registered embossing, spot UV lacquer on matt backgrounds, soft-touch lamination, and multi-layer varnish effects.

Bespoke Gift Packaging

From single-unit gift boxes for luxury hotels to seasonal gift-set packaging for retail, we engineer and produce complete packaging solutions — including printed tissue paper, ribbon, and branded bag components.

FAQ

Common Questions

What luxury finishing options does Aleph offer?

+

We offer hot-foil stamping (gold, silver, custom colours), blind and registered embossing, spot UV lacquer, soft-touch lamination, and multi-layer gloss/matt varnish combinations.

Can Aleph produce rigid boxes with magnetic closures?

+

Yes — we produce rigid lid-and-base and clamshell boxes with integrated magnetic closures, ribbon lifts, and custom internal trays or foam inserts.

What is the minimum order for luxury rigid boxes?

+

Rigid luxury boxes typically start from 200–500 units depending on complexity. For very bespoke structures, we recommend a consultation to discuss tooling and unit economics.

Do you produce seasonal gift packaging for hospitality brands?

+

Yes — we work with Lebanese hotels, restaurants, and hospitality groups to produce seasonal gift sets, Ramadan packaging, Christmas gift boxes, and corporate gifting solutions.

Can you match the exact paper and board weight for our existing packaging?

+

We can source and match specific board weights, paper textures, and surface finishes to maintain consistency with your existing packaging across new runs.
